1.Check WiFi Signal & Link Speed
Command: netsh wlan show interfaces
Look for: "Signal" (Percentage).
> 80%: Good for VPN.
< 60%: Expect RDP lag and "screen freezes."


2. Test for "Jitter" (Ping Stability)
RDP hates "Jitter"—when one packet takes 20ms and the next takes 200ms.
Command: ping -n 50 172.20.10.1
(Find your Gateway IP by typing ipconfig , it's usually something like  172.20.10.1)
The Red Flag: If you see "Request timed out" or jumps from 2ms to 100ms+, your Wi-Fi is the problem, not the VPN.


3.Direct GlobalProtect Check (The Tunnel Test)
command: ping -n 20 10.95.6.96
Evaluation:

< 100ms: RDP should be usable.
100ms - 200ms: Noticeable lag; typing feels "heavy."
> 250ms: RDP will be nearly unusable for productive work.


4.Test Latency to Your RDP Server
command: ping 10.95.6.96 -t

✅ Good: <50ms** | ⚠️ Slow: **>100ms | ❌ Problem: packet loss >1%


5.Check for Network Hops & Packet Loss
pathping 20 10.95.6.96
